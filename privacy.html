Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Hamzify Android app (com.hamzify.app) and this website.

Hamzify is a social app for sharing photos, videos, reels and stories and for messaging friends. This policy explains what information Hamzify stores, why, and how you can delete it. Hamzify does not show ads, does not sell your data and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. Information we store

Account information (needed to create and run your account):

  • Username, display name and email address
  • Password — stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text
  • Login sessions (a random token per signed-in device, with its expiry date)

Profile information you choose to add: profile photo, bio, links, pronouns, gender, profile banners, profile music, account type and category, and — only if you enter them under Personal information — phone number, contact email/phone for a business profile, and date of birth.

Content you create:

  • Posts, reels and stories: photos, videos, captions, hashtags, tagged people, the place name you type, music choice, stickers, polls and answers
  • Comments, likes, saves and collections, reposts, highlights, notes
  • Follows, follow requests, close friends, favourites, blocks, mutes and restricts

Messages: the text, photos, videos and voice messages you send in direct and group chats, reactions, replies, edits, pins, and whether a message has been read. Messages are stored on our server so they can be delivered to the other person and shown on all your devices. They are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) but are not end-to-end encrypted.

Activity information: your last-active time (updated at most about once a minute while the app is open), used to show the green "Active now" dot and "Active 5m ago" to people you chat with; story views and reel views; notifications about likes, comments and follows.

Verification (optional): if you apply for the verified badge, we store your legal name, a photo of your CNIC/ID, a selfie, the payment method, transaction ID and receipt image, and the decision. These are used only to review your request. Payment itself happens in JazzCash/Easypaisa — Hamzify never receives card or wallet login details.

Reports and appeals: if you report content or appeal a suspension, we store the report/appeal and its outcome.

Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to rate-limit abuse (for example too many login attempts). Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera — to take photos/videos for posts, stories and chats (only when you use the camera)
  • Microphone — to record videos and voice messages (only while recording)
  • Notifications — to alert you about messages and activity
  • Photos/storage — only the files you pick to upload

Hamzify does not collect your GPS location, contacts, call logs or SMS.

3. How we use information

  • To run the app: show your profile and posts, deliver messages, show online status and notifications
  • To keep Hamzify safe: prevent spam and abuse, review reports, enforce our rules
  • To review verification requests

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the hosting/infrastructure provider that stores the data on our behalf, or when required by law.

4. Who can see your information

  • Public accounts: your profile, posts, reels and stories can be seen by other Hamzify users. Private accounts: only approved followers see your posts and stories.
  • Messages are visible only to the people in that chat.
  • Your email, phone number, date of birth, password and verification documents are never shown on your profile (a business contact email/phone is shown only if you turn it on).
  • Hamzify administrators can access data when needed to review reports, appeals, verification requests or to keep the service safe.

5. Data retention

We keep your information while your account exists. Stories expire from view after 24 hours (unless you add them to a highlight). Vanish-mode messages disappear as described in the app. When you delete a post, story or message, it is removed from our database. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open your profile → menu (☰) → Delete account → enter your password to confirm.

This permanently and immediately deletes: your account and profile, all your posts, reels, stories and highlights and their photos/videos, your comments and likes, your direct messages (both sides of 1-to-1 chats), messages you sent in group chats, follows, blocks and other relationships, notifications, sessions, uploads, verification documents and appeals. Deleted accounts cannot be recovered.

Can't open the app? Email hamzaafzal1306@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Hamzify account" and your username. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Reports you submitted about other users may be kept for safety records without your profile. Server security logs expire automatically.

7. This website

This website (hamzify-official.sites.tab.bot) does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising, and it has no forms that collect personal information. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security.

8. Children

Hamzify is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS. Passwords are salted and hashed. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
